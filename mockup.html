Weekly timer

Enabled — up to 2 ON/OFF periods per day. At ON time the unit turns on at the period's speed; at OFF time it turns off (R9).
Time comes from NTP over Ethernet (time zone Europe/Kyiv). If the time is unknown, the timer does nothing. Do not run the wall controller's own weekly timer at the same time.

Heat kept vs lost

Heat recovery efficiency heating hours only; bypass and defrost excluded

Prices

Heat price (what 1 kWh of heating costs you) ₴/kWh
Electricity price (fans) ₴/kWh
Calculated every poll from the four temperatures and the current fan speeds (R10/R11):
kept = 0.335 × exhaust airflow × (room − exhaust)
lost = 0.335 × supply airflow × (room − outdoor) − kept
Only sensible heat. Moisture recovery is not counted, so real savings are a bit higher.
